What went wrong last time

From April to June your weight held at 186–188 lb. The plan wasn't producing a deficit in practice. Its weekday menus were aggressive (1,600–1,900 kcal), weekends made up the gap, and running three cuisines with Sunday prep plus three cook nights was a lot to keep up while running a household on your own. From July, weigh-ins dropped from ~25 a month to 3, and weight went up 6 lb.

The protein habit worked, and your strength shows it. That part stays.

Old plan

  • 3 rotating cuisines, each with its own recipes
  • Sunday prep + cook nights Mon, Tue, Thu
  • Gram-level quantities for everything
  • Big deficit, 18-week deadline

This plan

  • One cooking method; only the seasoning and sauces change
  • One ~50 min cook on Sunday, no weeknight cooking
  • Portions set by the plan, not by counting
  • Small deficit, no deadline

How it works

  • Weekdays run on defaults. Breakfast, snack, lunch and dinner are the same shape every day, so you never decide what to eat Monday–Friday.
  • The deficit comes from the defaults. A default weekday lands around 2,100 kcal, a climb day around 2,350, and weekends run looser. That averages near 2,300, roughly 400–500 under what you likely burn. In practice that shows up as about 0.5 lb/week.
  • Boredom is handled by sauce, not by recipes. Chicken and ground meat get cooked the same way every week. A 4-week flavor rotation changes the seasoning and the two sauces.
  • Weekends are yours, with two rules. Keep breakfast, and put protein in every meal.
  • Adjust monthly, not daily. A cheap scale jumps around, so you look at the month, a tape measure and a photo.

Expectation: at 0.5 lb/week, visible abs probably take 6–9 months. There's no deadline, so a slow month isn't a failure. The only thing that counts is still running this in March.

Break-glass meals

When you skipped the cook, got home late, or can't face another bowl. These count as on plan.

Rotisserie chicken bowl

~200 g rotisserie meat, a 250 g microwave rice pouch (use ~175 g), bagged salad.

Eggs + toast + cottage cheese

3 eggs, 2 slices toast, a ~150 g cottage cheese cup. Ten minutes.

Burrito-bowl place

Double meat, rice, beans, salsa. Skip either cheese or sour cream.

Frozen protein meal + shake

Pick one with 30g+ protein. Keep two in the freezer as insurance.

The Sunday cook

The same five steps every week. Only the seasoning changes; see Flavors for this week's. Makes 11 bowls: Sunday dinner through Friday dinner.

Gear

Rice cooker · 2 foil-lined sheet pans (foil means no scrubbing) · 1 big skillet · 2 large fridge containers · 4 gallon freezer bags

  1. Start the rice720 g / 1.6 lb dry rice in the rice cooker (about 4 cups). It finishes on its own.
    0:00
  2. Chicken in the ovenOven to 425°F / 220°C. 1.4 kg / 3 lb boneless thighs on the foil-lined pans, a spray of oil, this week's chicken seasoning, toss by hand. Roast 25–30 min, to 175°F / 80°C inside.
    0:05
  3. Brown the ground meat1.4 kg / 3 lb ground beef (93% lean) or turkey in the skillet over medium-high. Break it up, cook ~10 min until no pink, drain if needed, stir in this week's meat seasoning.
    0:10
  4. CoolLet everything sit ~15 min. Clean the skillet now and throw the foil away.
    0:35
  5. Weigh, then split fridge / freezerWeigh each cooked total and divide to get this week's portions (cooked yields vary, so this beats a fixed number). Then put half of each into a fridge container and half into a flat freezer bag. Fridge covers Sun–Wed lunch, freezer covers Wed dinner–Fri.
    0:50 · done

Portion math

Chicken · 5 lunches
Cooked total ÷ 5
typical: ~950 g → 190 g
Ground meat · 6 dinners
Cooked total ÷ 6
typical: ~1,020 g → 170 g
Rice · 11 bowls + 2 climb extras
Cooked total ÷ 12
typical: ~2,100 g → 175 g (260 g climb nights)
Tip
Write the three numbers on masking tape on each container.

Why freeze half: cooked meat and rice keep 3–4 days in the fridge. Freezing half means Friday's food is as good as Monday's.

Diner week: potatoes replace rice at dinner. After the chicken comes out, roast 1.5 kg / 3.3 lb frozen diced potatoes on the pans for 25 min, weigh, divide by 6. Cook only 360 g / 0.8 lb dry rice for lunches.

Four-week flavor rotation

Same cook, different week. All sauces are store-bought or two-ingredient mixes, so nothing gets chopped.

Swap-ins when a week gets old

Replace any week with one of these. Same method.

Tikka

Chicken: garam masala + salt. Meat: stir a jar of tikka masala sauce into the browned meat. Sauce: plain yogurt + mango chutney.

BBQ

Chicken: BBQ rub. Meat: BBQ sauce stirred in. Sauces: BBQ sauce, coleslaw kit on the side.

Italian

Chicken: Italian seasoning + garlic. Meat: jar of marinara stirred in. Sauces: pesto, parmesan. Swap rice for pasta.

Peanut satay

Chicken: soy + curry powder. Meat: soy + ginger. Sauces: bottled peanut sauce, sweet chili.

Groceries

Staples are the same every week. Save them as a grocery pickup order and one chore disappears.

How to know it's working

No logging. The scale is a $20 bioimpedance unit, so ignore its body-fat and muscle readings. Those are estimated from your weight and move with it. Weight trend, waist and photos are what count.

Weekly

Step on the scale most mornings you remember, after the bathroom. Aim for 3+ times a week. Don't react to any single number.

First Sunday of each month

Tape around your waist at the belly button, relaxed, exhaled. Front and side photo in the same spot and light. Then look at the month's average weight and pick one row below.

Down 1–3 lb, climbing feels good
Change nothing. This is the plan working.
Flat for the month, but you mostly followed it
Make one change: drop the evening cup, or cut dinner rice to 130 g. Only one.
Down more than 4 lb, or climbing feels flat
Add rice back: 260 g at lunch on climb days too.
Up, or flat and the plan slipped
Don't cut food. Figure out which default broke (usually the Sunday cook) and fix that.
Every ~12 weeks
Take a maintenance week: keep the structure, add the evening cup every day and 260 g rice in every bowl. Then pick back up.

A good week is 8 of 10 weekday bowls from the batch. Weekends don't make or break it.

When you get there: maintenance is this same plan with the extra rice and evening cup kept in.
